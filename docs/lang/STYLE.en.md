# Voice and writing guide — English

Used for GitHub READMEs, itch.io pages, LinkedIn posts in English, and a future EN version of the site.
Goal: it should read like Gabriel wrote it, not like a generated template. Same person as `STYLE.es.md`, different language.

## 1. Who is speaking

Gabriel, first person singular. A Unity developer from Lima, Peru, who ships games, gamified training and small tools. Direct, specific, no corporate voice.

- "I build", "I shipped", "I sold it in 2017", "it broke, I fixed it". Never "our team", "we at Thonking".
- Name real things: 3S Design, MINEDU, itch.io, MetaQuest 3, 6,221 downloads. Specifics are what sound human.
- Admit limits. "Cancelled, but with real progress" beats "successfully delivered".
- Spanish proper nouns stay as they are (Lima, Perú with accent is fine, "Te la creíste" keeps its title).

## 2. Register

- Plain international English. Not British spelling, not startup-speak.
- Technical terms stay lowercase when common (build, deploy, playtest, cleaner).
- No emojis in final copy. No exclamation marks unless quoting someone.
- Numbers with thousands separators (6,221). "20k+" only in visual stat blocks.
- Peruvian flavor is allowed when it explains something ("floro", roughly "empty talk"), never as decoration.

## 3. Tells of generated text (banned)

If one of these shows up, rewrite the sentence.

**Structure**
- The contrast frame: "It's not X, it's Y." / "This isn't about X, it's about Y."
- Automatic triads: three adjectives, three bullets, three benefits, always three.
- Closing with a rhetorical question or "Ready to...?".
- Opening with "In today's world..." / "In an era of..." / "Whether you're a... or a...".
- A paragraph that summarizes the previous paragraph. Say it once.
- Bullet lists where every item starts with a bold word and a colon, all the same length.
- Em dashes as a crutch in every sentence. One per paragraph at most; a period is usually better.

**Vocabulary**
- Elevate, empower, unlock, supercharge, transform, revolutionize, redefine, streamline, leverage.
- Seamless, robust, scalable, cutting-edge, innovative, disruptive, holistic, comprehensive, end-to-end.
- Delve, dive in, explore, discover (as marketing imperatives), journey, landscape, tapestry.
- "Immersive experience", "next level", "game-changer", "best-in-class".
- "Passionate about", "enthusiast", "lover of".
- Filler adverbs: truly, really, incredibly, remarkably, effortlessly.
- "And much more", "and so on", "etc." to pad.

**Tone**
- Uniform enthusiasm. Human writing has flat parts.
- Perfect symmetry between sections (title + 2 lines + 3 bullets, repeated).
- No dates, no names, no numbers. Vague is what reads artificial.

## 4. How we write here

- Short sentences mixed with the occasional long one. Uneven rhythm on purpose.
- One paragraph, one idea. Two ideas, two paragraphs.
- Lead with the verb when possible: "Built", "Published", "Sold in 2017".
- Detail over adjective. Instead of "a powerful tool": "turns 400 lines of text into OGG files in one run".
- Sentence case for headings, never Title Case. All caps only as a visual device in the design.
- Round figures get a "+", not a repeated "more than".

## 5. Examples

| Reads like a template | Reads like Gabriel |
|---|---|
| I'm a passionate Unity developer with extensive experience crafting immersive experiences. | I've been making games with Unity for 6 years. Started as a tester at 3S Design in 2020. |
| GGleaner isn't just a cleaner, it's your ally for an optimized system. | GGleaner deletes the junk Unity, Unreal, Godot and Docker leave behind. Open source, Windows. |
| Discover how to take your training to the next level! | A training video game for a top-3 retailer in Mexico. 20,000+ employees. Also in VR. |
| Explore my projects and dive into my creative world. | What I ship. |

## 6. Fixed strings

- Tagline: "I make people learn by playing." (working translation of "Hago que la gente aprenda jugando.")
- Footer credit: "Mascot illustrated by @floruwu_art · Made with care in Lima"
- Contact: "I reply within 24-48h · Lima, Peru (GMT-5)"
- Enterprise client: "a top-3 retailer in Mexico" or "client under NDA". Never the name.

## 7. Pre-publish checklist

1. Any word from section 3? Rewrite.
2. Does every claim have a name, a date or a number nearby? If not, is it needed?
3. Would Gabriel say this out loud to another dev? If it sounds like a landing page, cut it.
4. No emojis, no exclamation marks, no Title Case.
